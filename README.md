**Project Overview – Garage Management System (GMS)**

**1. Introduction**
The Garage Management System (GMS) is a cloud-based application developed using Salesforce CRM to automate and streamline daily operations in automobile service centers and garages. It eliminates manual work in managing customers, vehicles, services, inventory, and billing. The system ensures accuracy, efficiency, and customer satisfaction by integrating automation tools, workflows, and dashboards within the Salesforce platform.

**2. Purpose of the Project**
The main purpose of the GMS is to create a centralized and intelligent garage management platform that replaces paper-based and error-prone manual systems. It enables garages to efficiently handle customer service requests, track vehicle servicing, manage spare parts inventory, and generate automated bills — all within a single, secure cloud environment.

**3. Problem Statement**
Traditional garages often rely on manual operations such as maintaining handwritten job cards, tracking inventory manually, and generating physical invoices. These outdated methods lead to inefficiencies, errors, and customer dissatisfaction. There is a need for a digital solution that automates every stage of the service cycle while maintaining transparency and reliability.

**4. Objectives**
To automate the workflow of garage operations including customer management, service booking, and billing.
To maintain accurate customer and vehicle service histories for future reference.
To enable real-time service tracking for customers and staff.
To manage inventory efficiently and avoid stock shortages.
To reduce paperwork and human errors.
To provide analytical dashboards and performance reports.
To ensure secure, scalable, and cloud-based data storage using Salesforce.

**5. System Architecture**
The system is designed based on Salesforce’s multi-tenant cloud model and follows a client–server architecture:
User Interface Layer: Created using Lightning Pages and Components for easy interaction.
Application Logic Layer: Built using Apex Classes, Flows, and Validation Rules for automation.
Database Layer: Managed using Salesforce Custom Objects like Customer, Vehicle, Service Request, Job Card, and Billing.
**
6. Key Functional Modules**

Customer Management – Register customers and their vehicles.
Appointment Scheduling – Create and manage service bookings.
Service Request Handling – Assign mechanics and track ongoing work.
Inventory Management – Track spare parts and update stock automatically.
Billing and Feedback – Generate invoices and collect customer feedback.
Reports and Dashboards – Monitor service performance and revenue insights.

**7. Technologies Used**

Salesforce Lightning Platform – For UI and application development.
Apex Programming – For backend logic and automation.
Workflow Rules & Flows – For process automation and notifications.
Custom Objects & Relationships – For structured data storage.
Reports & Dashboards – For data visualization and business analytics.

**8. Workflow Description**

Customer Registration: A new customer record is created in Salesforce.
Vehicle Addition: Vehicle details are linked to the customer.
Service Request: The system logs the service complaint and assigns it to a mechanic.
Job Card Generation: Details of repair tasks, costs, and technician assignment are stored.
Inventory Update: Spare parts used are automatically deducted from the stock.
Billing & Invoice: The bill is generated upon service completion.
Reports & Dashboards: Admins can view summaries of services, payments, and feedback.

**9. Expected Outcomes**

Complete automation of garage operations.
Paperless workflow and reduced service turnaround time.
Transparent billing and real-time service updates.
Accurate tracking of inventory and services.
Increased customer satisfaction through better communication.
Real-time performance insights via Salesforce dashboards.

**10. Advantages**

Fully cloud-based and accessible from anywhere.
Secure and customizable using Salesforce features.
Reduces manual work and minimizes human error.
Compatible with the Salesforce mobile app for instant access.
Scalable for multi-branch garage networks.

**11. Future Enhancements**

Integration with IoT sensors to detect vehicle conditions automatically.
AI-based service prediction using Salesforce Einstein.
Integration with SMS or WhatsApp for customer updates.
Online booking portal through Salesforce Experience Cloud.

**12. Conclusion**

The Garage Management System (GMS) demonstrates how Salesforce CRM can be customized beyond sales applications to automate complex service-based industries. The project digitizes garage operations by integrating customers, vehicles, and billing under one platform. Through automation tools, secure cloud storage, and interactive dashboards, GMS enhances transparency, efficiency, and customer trust.
